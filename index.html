The Project

Ambient acoustic soundscapes, dappled with subtle electronic touches — music for early mornings, long drives, and the quiet space between words.

North Boundary Dispatch is an ambient project from Oak Ridge, Tennessee. Guitar and field-recording quiet, slowly unfolding — haunting, melancholy, and unhurried. Pieces built to sit beneath the day and color it from underneath.
